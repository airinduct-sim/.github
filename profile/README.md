AirInduct Sim takes a wireless charging link from coil geometry to a working design.
Engineers, researchers and students use it to compute mutual inductance, coupling and
induced voltage, explore 3D fields, and check efficiency, heating and exposure before
building hardware.

**What's in the product**

- 8 coil topologies: square and circular Helmholtz and DTC pairs
- Optimizer and rectifier design
- Link designer, tolerance, thermal, ferrite and foreign-object analysis
- Field exposure checks against ICNIRP limits
- Coil library, run history, and team workspaces for universities and companies
- Exports to CSV, Excel, Word, PDF, Touchstone and SPICE
